## ☁️ OneDrive for Business (Cloud Storage)
* **Data Integrity:** Ensured critical business documents were correctly synchronized to the cloud for backup and redundancy.
* **Access Control:** Managed file permissions and generated secure share links to replace large email attachments.
* **Sync Troubleshooting:** Resolved file conflict errors and synchronization delays on local workstations.
